Get to Know Middlesex

Middlesex is an independent school in Concord, Massachusetts, serving boarding and day students in grades 9 through 12. Students and faculty share a close-knit campus community not far from Boston.

Learning Together

Students exchange ideas in small classes and explore a broad range of subjects.

A Connected Community

Faculty support students in the classroom and throughout campus life.

Room to Explore

Arts, athletics, and student activities offer opportunities to try something new.

Learning Beyond the Classroom

At Middlesex, teachers and students connect through classroom conversations, shared meals, and activities across campus. The school encourages students to think deeply, care for others, and explore their interests.

Life on Campus

From performances and student gatherings to practices and games, campus life brings people together in many different ways. Students have space to pursue familiar interests and discover new ones.
